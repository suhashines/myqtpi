My dear myqtpi,

Happy birthday. I have been trying to gather our memories into one place, but the problem is that our life keeps producing more of them.

There is the grand version of us: four years of dating, an engagement at your home, and the feeling that the person I have loved for so long is becoming my future wife. I still catch myself looking at that photograph and thinking how stunning you looked, and how quietly enormous that moment was.

But there is also the version I love most: you making a heart out of shiuli flowers, us talking behind Architecture until the world becomes unimportant, walking through Saint Martin, copying a pose from Before Sunrise, playing chess while I fail because my opponent is my queen, or putting your monkey cap on me and deciding I look cute.

I do not need every day with you to be exciting. I want the ordinary days too. The dull days. The tea days. The days where we talk about art, films, books, work, food, fear, and everything in between. I want the life that keeps happening after the photographs are taken.

Maybe that is what it means to understand someone: to keep noticing them in all their forms, and to keep choosing them in all the ordinary moments. I choose you, pakhii. I would choose you again.

With all my love,
Suhas
