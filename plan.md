# myqtpi — Birthday Site Plan

## 1. Purpose

Create a small, self-contained birthday website for Romana's birthday on November 5, 2026.

The site should feel like a private love letter disguised as a playful little digital object: minimal, meaningful, romantic, funny, and unmistakably personal.

Primary emotional goal:

> Make Romana laugh first, then feel deeply seen and loved.

## 2. Creative direction

Working title: `myqtpi`

The name comes from the couple's math joke/meme: `u = qtπ`.

Visual and emotional posture:

- Warm, intimate, and slightly handmade
- Romantic without becoming overly ornamental
- Funny without turning the relationship into a joke
- Specific rather than generic
- Quietly literary and artistic
- More like a personal digital keepsake than a conventional birthday landing page

Primary surface: **Explore**

The visitor should move through a small collection of memories, jokes, images, and messages. A secondary **Decide/Learn** layer appears in the opening and closing moments: the site gradually reveals why Romana matters to Suhas.

## 3. Core experience

The site opens with a compact fake-system interface:

- `myqtpi.exe` or `myqtpi // birthday edition`
- Status line: `Romana birthday update detected`
- Small playful loading sequence
- Main action: `Open birthday package`

After opening, the page becomes a vertical, editorial memory trail rather than a dashboard or card grid.

Suggested flow:

1. Birthday opening
2. Funny system/app introduction
3. Curated relationship memories
4. A small interactive joke or quiz
5. A sincere closing letter
6. Final birthday wish and replay option

A tiny relationship quiz with 3–5 questions.

The quiz should be affectionate rather than testing factual memory. Every result should lead to a loving or funny response.

- What does Suhas want to do even when life is boring?

Final quiz result:

> Correct. Romana is always right today. On other days too, probably.

This interaction is optional if it makes the page feel too busy. The memory trail and final letter have priority.

### Section F — “The important part”

A sincere closing letter from Suhas.

This should be the emotional center of the final third of the site. It should mention, in natural language:

- Four years of dating and the engagement
- The wish to share ordinary life, not only exciting moments
- Being understood as a form of love

- How Romana has made ordinary moments feel worth remembering

The final letter should be written in Suhas's voice, not in generic romantic prose.

- Optional small line: `I would choose you again. Even if you are holding the chess queen.`

## 6. Visual system

Suggested direction:

- Background: warm paper/cream or very dark brown-black, but not both everywhere
- Accent: one romantic coral/red or muted rose
- Secondary accent: soft shiuli orange or faded sky blue
- Text: warm near-black with a muted secondary tone
- Borders: thin, understated, slightly imperfect where appropriate
- Corners: mostly restrained; avoid making every element a rounded card
- Typography: editorial serif or humanist display for emotional lines, precise sans for interface text, small monospace for system jokes

Avoid:

- Large gradient hero backgrounds
- Generic SaaS cards
- Excessive glassmorphism
- Heart/emoji decoration everywhere
- Stock imagery
- Fake metrics or generic “love dashboard” statistics
- Avoid autoplay music unless explicitly enabled by the visitor. The site should attempt to start the selected romantic track after the visitor's first interaction, provide a visible music toggle and volume control, shuffle the two romantic tracks during the memory journey, and switch to `birthday-music.mp3` at the final wishing moment. If playback is blocked, show a clear play control and continue normally without sound.
- A dense collage of all 47 images

## 7. AI-slop guardrails

The site must feel like a specific private artifact, not a generic AI-generated romantic landing page.

Design rules:

- Commit to the mixed-order editorial memory trail before styling individual components.
- Avoid the default centered hero followed by three equal cards.
- Avoid generic SaaS cards, feature grids, dashboard metrics, badges, and decorative icon toppers.
- Use one coherent palette with deliberate contrast rather than gradients everywhere.
- Let typography, image scale, spacing, and sequencing carry hierarchy before adding containers or decoration.
- Use supplied photographs, memes, and illustrations as the visual language; do not use stock imagery or invented illustrations.
- Keep jokes specific to Suhas and Romana: BUET, Shilpakala, chess queen, shiuli, Chattogram, Saint Martin, the monkey cap, gym flexing, and their shared media references.
- Do not fill empty space with generic romantic slogans, fake statistics, excessive hearts, or emoji.
- Keep motion purposeful: reveal memories, support transitions, and create a festive atmosphere without turning the page into a spectacle.
- Perform an explicit anti-slop review after implementation and revise any generic composition before delivery.

## 8. Interaction and accessibility

Required:

- Fully responsive on phone and desktop
- Buttons and interactive elements at least 44px tall
- Keyboard-accessible controls
- Visible focus states
- Alt text for every meaningful image
- `prefers-reduced-motion` support
- No interaction required to read the sincere letter
- Audio starts only after a visitor interaction; no forced autoplay

Optional:

- Small sound toggle only if a suitable local audio file is later provided
- LocalStorage to remember whether the opening animation has been seen
- A hidden “reveal all memories” mode if the site remains uncluttered

## 8. Technical plan

Build a standalone local HTML artifact first.

Target files:

- `index.html` — complete birthday site
- `memory-notes.md` — source memory archive
- `images/` — supplied images
- `plan.md` — this plan

Implementation preferences:

- Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
- No build step unless needed
- CSS variables for the visual system
- Local relative image paths
- No external dependencies by default
- Use semantic sections and meaningful headings
- Keep the main page easy to open directly in a browser

## 9. Content safeguards

- Do not invent dates, places, dialogue, or personality traits.
- Preserve the distinction between personal photographs, memes, Puuung illustrations, and film/book references.
- Use “April 4, 2026” only if the date is intended to follow the established current-year convention.
- Keep the engagement date exact: October 22, 2026.
- Preserve the quote/theme about understanding carefully; use short quotations and attribute them appropriately if included.
- Do not expose private material beyond what Suhas deliberately placed in the image folder.
